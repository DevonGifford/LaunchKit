LaunchKit runs this website. This policy explains what personal data it
handles and why. In short: there are no analytics, advertising or tracking
cookies, and we only see what you choose to send us.

## Who is responsible

LaunchKit is responsible for the personal data described here. For anything
about this policy or your data, get in touch through the
[contact page](/contact).

## What we collect

### When you browse

Our hosting provider records technical details of each request, such as your
IP address, browser, the page you asked for and the time, so it can deliver
the website and keep it secure.

The website itself runs no analytics, and loads no third-party scripts, fonts
or embeds: every page and asset comes from this website.

### When you send a message

The contact form asks for your name, email address, a topic and your message,
and for your agreement to be contacted. We use these details only to reply to
your enquiry. We don't add you to a mailing list, use them for marketing, or
share them with anyone else.

### Preferences kept on your device

- **Language:** when you choose a language, a cookie named `NEXT_LOCALE`
  remembers it, so the next page opens in the same language. It's deleted when
  you close your browser.
- **Appearance:** when you choose light or dark mode, your choice is saved in
  your browser's local storage as `theme`. It stays on your device and is
  never sent to us.

Both exist only for features you ask for, and neither is used to track you.

## Why we're allowed to use it

- **Replying to your message:** your agreement, given with the checkbox on the
  contact form, and our interest in answering the people who write to us.
- **Server records:** our interest in running a reliable, secure website.

## How long we keep it

We keep your message only as long as we need it to handle your enquiry, then
delete it. Server records are kept for the period our hosting provider sets,
and then deleted.

## Who else is involved

We don't sell personal data or share it for anyone else's purposes. The
companies that host this website and carry our email process data on our
behalf, only to provide those services.

## Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you,
to limit how we use it, or to give you a copy. If you agreed to be contacted,
you can withdraw that agreement at any time. To do any of these, write to us
through the [contact page](/contact).

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can also complain to
your local data protection authority.

## Other websites

Links to other websites, such as GitHub, lead to services with their own
privacy policies, which we don't control.

## Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
