LaunchKit runs this website. This policy explains what personal data it
handles and why. In short: there's no advertising and no tracking cookies. We
count visits and measure page speed without identifying you, and otherwise only
see what you choose to send us.

## Who is responsible

LaunchKit is responsible for the personal data described here. For anything
about this policy or your data, get in touch through the
[contact page](/contact).

## What we collect

### When you browse

Our hosting provider records technical details of each request, such as your
IP address, browser, the page you asked for and the time, so it can deliver
the website and keep it secure.

The website loads no third-party fonts or embeds. Every page, asset and script
comes from this website, including the two measurement services below.

### Measuring visits and speed

Vercel, our hosting provider, runs two services for us that show how the
website is used and how quickly it loads. Their scripts come from this website,
so they set no third-party cookies, and neither lets us identify you or follow
you across other websites.

- **Vercel Web Analytics** counts page views. With each one it records the
  page's address, the page you came from, your country, region and city, your
  browser, operating system and type of device, and the time. It tells visits
  apart with a code calculated from your request, which it discards after 24
  hours. See
  [Vercel's description](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy).
- **Vercel Speed Insights** measures how quickly pages load and respond. With
  each measurement it records the page's address, the speed of your network,
  your browser, operating system and type of device, and your country. It keeps
  nothing that identifies you or links one page you visit to the next. See
  [Vercel's description](https://vercel.com/docs/speed-insights/privacy-policy).

Because neither identifies you, both run for every visitor. Other analytics or
marketing services would run only if you allowed them in the website's privacy
settings; this website doesn't use any at the moment.

### When you send a message

The contact form asks for your name, email address, a topic and your message,
and for your agreement to be contacted. We use these details only to reply to
your enquiry. We don't add you to a mailing list, use them for marketing, or
share them with anyone else.

### Preferences kept on your device

- **Language:** when you choose a language, a cookie named `NEXT_LOCALE`
  remembers it, so the next page opens in the same language. It's deleted when
  you close your browser.
- **Appearance:** when you choose light or dark mode, your choice is saved in
  your browser's local storage as `theme`. It stays on your device and is
  never sent to us.
- **Privacy choices:** when you answer the privacy prompt, your answer is saved
  in your browser's local storage as `consent`, so we don't ask on every page.
  It stays on your device and is never sent to us. To change it, use **Privacy
  settings** at the bottom of any page.

Each exists only for something you ask for, and none is used to track you.

## Why we're allowed to use it

- **Replying to your message:** your agreement, given with the checkbox on the
  contact form, and our interest in answering the people who write to us.
- **Server records:** our interest in running a reliable, secure website.
- **Measuring visits and speed:** our interest in understanding how the website
  is used and keeping it fast, using data that doesn't identify you.

## How long we keep it

We keep your message only as long as we need it to handle your enquiry, then
delete it. Server records are kept for the period our hosting provider sets,
and then deleted. Web Analytics discards the code that tells visits apart after
24 hours; Vercel keeps the measurements themselves for the period it sets.

## Who else is involved

We don't sell personal data or share it for anyone else's purposes. The
companies that host this website and carry our email process data on our
behalf, only to provide those services. Vercel, which hosts the website, also
runs Web Analytics and Speed Insights for us.

## Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you,
to limit how we use it, or to give you a copy. If you agreed to be contacted,
you can withdraw that agreement at any time. To do any of these, write to us
through the [contact page](/contact).

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can also complain to
your local data protection authority.

## Other websites

Links to other websites, such as GitHub, lead to services with their own
privacy policies, which we don't control.

## Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
